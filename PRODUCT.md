# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: React + Vite + TypeScript, Three.js through React Three Fiber and Drei, Motion for interface transitions, and custom CSS. This supports a small interactive WebGL world with place-based portfolio content.

## Users

Recruiters evaluating a creative developer's work and approach.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that helps a visitor understand the maker's work, explore selected projects, and find a way to make contact. This first version uses a fictional developer and fictional project details as temporary demo content; the owner plans to supply real information later.

## Positioning

The portfolio is a small open world driven by a car. Driving to the garage, studio, signal tower, or post office opens the corresponding projects, profile, skills, or contact section. Personal positioning remains open until the owner provides real information.

## Operating Context

Recruiters primarily scan on desktop and may also open the portfolio on a phone. They need to identify the creator, inspect relevant work, and find contact information without learning a complicated 3D interface.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Interactive 3D open world with a drivable car, right-mouse camera orbit, place-based content panels, and direct accessible navigation.
- Responsive web experience with touch steering, direct accessible navigation, and reduced-motion support.
- Fictional name, biography, project names, metrics, and contact details must be visibly identified as demo content until replaced.
- No external project or client claims are established.

## Brand Commitments

- Use a small explorable world as the portfolio's organizing concept.
- Do not use gradients.

## Evidence on Hand

- No real personal details, work samples, clients, credentials, or contact information have been provided yet.
- Any content shown before those materials arrive is fictional demonstration content.

## Product Principles

- Use the explorable world as the main navigation, while keeping direct access to the work visible for recruiters.
- Make driving optional; accessible navigation buttons and on-screen touch controls open the same places.
- Mark fictional content clearly and replace it with supplied facts when available.
- Keep the experience responsive and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.
